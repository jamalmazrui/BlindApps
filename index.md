## Introduction {#introduction}

This directory lists 90 apps and tools made by 39 developers who are blind or have low vision. It is a companion to [Blind Developers][blind-developers], which profiles the people. This page puts the apps first, so you can find a tool by name, platform, or purpose.

An app is included when all of the following are true, the same tests Blind Developers uses:

- The developer is blind or has low vision.
- The developer created or led the app. Team software counts when the developer was a creator or a development lead.
- A working public link leads to where the app can be obtained and used.
- The app has been actively developed or maintained in 2020 or later.

Blind Developers shows up to three apps per person. This directory has no such limit: it includes every app that meets the tests and has a verified link.

Each app has a heading that links to where you can get it, a one-sentence description, and fields in alphabetical order: Available from, Category, Developer, Platform, and Tech, the last naming the programming languages, other key technology, and any AI involved, where known; an AI marked "(to build)" helped make the app rather than running inside it. The Developer field links to that person's entry in Blind Developers. Apps are listed alphabetically by title, ignoring a leading A, An, or The, under a heading for each letter. Statistics come next, and appendixes group the apps by platform, by category, and by developer.

Each app belongs to its developer and keeps its own license. This directory's own text is available under [CC BY-SA 4.0][cc-by-sa]. It is English-language in scope. Its companions are [Blind Authors][blind-authors], [Blind Presenters][blind-presenters], and [Blind Creators][blind-creators].
